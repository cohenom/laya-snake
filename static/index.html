Laya Snake Arena connecting…

8 Snake games running in parallel, all driven by real forward passes of the Laya text/JSON decision model (ModernBERT-large classifier, not a game AI). Laya's own forward pass floors at ~100ms per decision no matter how many games are batched into it (measured — batching gave no speedup on this CPU), so one board gets a real Laya decision each tick, round-robin, while the rest move on a free local safety heuristic. That's why the highlighted board rotates. The most efficient boards accumulate score; the rest die and respawn — watch the leaderboard.
